A private guardian for the agent age

Let agents near your life.
Not into it.

Aegis is a translucent little guardian between your AI agents and the services that hold your music, photos, mail, and calendar. It holds the keys in its tentacles, lets you see everything it touches, and stings anything that reaches too far.

Anatomy of a guardian

Why a jellyfish?

Jellyfish have drifted through the oceans for half a billion years without a brain, a stomach, or a single opinion about your playlists. Aegis is built the same way.

Translucent

You can see right through it. The admin Map shows which agent touched which kind of data, and when. Nothing happens in the murk.

Tentacles hold the keys

Aegis owns every OAuth connection. Tokens stay encrypted on your disk. Agents never see a token and never get asked to log in.

A precise sting

Per-type permissions (none, freebusy, read, write) are enforced on every request. Reach too far and the answer is a polite 403.

No stomach

Aegis doesn't store your media. Photos stay in Immich or Google, songs stay on Spotify. It passes things along and keeps nothing for itself.

Use case 01Works today

Our Year, in Songs & Light

An anniversary mixtape that remembers what you were listening to, and what you were looking at.

Ask an agent for your own “Wrapped”, but for the two of you. Aegis pulls liked tracks from Spotify, YouTube Music, and Amazon Music into one list, merged by ISRC so the same song isn't counted three times. Then it lines them up with photo metadata from the same weeks. The agent puts it together. The photos never leave Immich or Google Photos.

  • GET /v1/library/tracks
  • GET /v1/library/photos
  • people[] on photos
  • ISRC-first merge

The player is set to none. The agent can read what you loved, but it can't start blasting music in your kitchen.

The Glass Calendar

A scheduling app can find you an hour. It can't read about your life.

Connect a scheduling app with one click on the Aegis icon. It gets a scoped app token that only sees free/busy: start, end, all-day, status. No titles, attendees, locations, or descriptions. The app knows when you're busy, never why. Flip the toggle off and the token is destroyed.

  • permissions: events → freebusy
  • GET /v1/library/freebusy
  • Bearer aeg_app_…
  • POST /v1/apps/revoke

Aegis does the redaction itself. Nobody is relying on a prompt asking nicely. Even /v1/library/events comes back with the details blanked.

Use case 03Works today

The Undertow

When you need to know what actually happened, and when, across every inbox you've ever had.

A landlord dispute, an insurance claim, “when did we actually leave?” Aegis keeps a compressed, searchable archive of your mail on your machine and pairs it with your calendar. An agent can rebuild a timeline from receipts, bookings, and cancellations without hammering live Gmail. It can also tell you what the evidence doesn't prove.

  • GET /v1/mail/search
  • GET /v1/library/events
  • local SQLite FTS
  • Takeout import

Search runs against the archive on your own disk. The agent gets a timeline. It never gets your inbox.

How it works

Two currents, one jellyfish

Aegis sits beside Tailscale Aperture, the gateway your agents already talk through. It's a single Go binary on your tailnet.

The hook current

Agent→Aperture→Aegis hook→LLM

Before a prompt reaches the model, Aegis spots the services you mentioned (“my Spotify”, “photos from June”, “my calendar”) and tells the agent where the safe door is: Aegis's own endpoints, with the permissions you set.

The proxy current

Agent→Aegis library→Spotify · Immich · Gmail · …

The agent calls /v1/library/* and gets back plain playlists, tracks, photos, events, and messages, merged across every connected service. Aegis attaches the tokens, caches only successful answers, and enforces every sting.

Fail-openIf Aegis hits a snag, your request goes through unchanged. Your agent doesn't stall.
Aegis owns OAuthOne encrypted token per connection, refreshed automatically. Agents never see it.
Media stays homeThe original stays with the source service. Aegis only handles metadata.
Tailnet onlyNo public Funnel. Only your own devices and people can reach it.
Surface

Release your own jellyfish

Aegis is open source. Build it, point it at your services, and keep it on your tailnet.

# build
git clone https://github.com/fudomunro/aegis.git && cd aegis
go build -o aegis .

# run it on your tailnet
./aegis -base-url https://aegis.your-tailnet.ts.net \
        -auth-mode tailnet -allowed-logins you@example.com \
        -token-pass "$AEGIS_TOKEN_PASS"
